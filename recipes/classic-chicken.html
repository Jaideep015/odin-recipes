Classic chicken stir fry recipe

Description

.

Tender strips of chicken and crisp vegetables come together in a glossy, savoury soy-garlic sauce - a fast flavorful dish that's ready in just 20 minutes.Perfect for a busy weeknight when you want something wholesome without spending hours in the kitchen.Each bite balances juicy chicken with crunchy veggies, finished with a nutty touch of sesame and a sprinkle of fresh green onions.

ingredients

  • 500g chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
  • 3 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon corn starch
  • 2 tablespoon water
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • 2 green onions, chopped

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, cornstarch and water to make the sauce. set aside.

    2. ,

  2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large pan and wok over high heat.Add chicken and cook util browned and cooked through, about 5-6 minutes.Remove and set aside.

  3. Add remaining oil to the pan.Add bell pepper, carrot and broccoli. stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.

  4. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.

  5. Return chicken to the pan and pour in the sauce.Toss everything together and cook for 2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats evenly.

  6. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions.Serve hot over rice
