garlic butter mushroom pasta recipe

Description

Golden, buttery mushrooms meet a rich garlic cream sauce in this comforting pasta dish that comes together in under 30 minutes. Perfect for quick weeknight dinner or when you ate craving something indulgent without much effort. The sauce is silky , and full of flavour, thanks to a generous hit of garlic, parmesan ad fresh thyme- finished with a sprinkle of parsley for a pop of freshness.

Ingredients

  • 400g pasta (fettucine or penne)
  • 3 tablespoon butter
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 300g mushroom, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 tablespoon thyme leaves grated
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1/2 tablespoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoon fresh parsley chopped

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente, according to package directions.Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining

  2. Heat butter and oil in a large skillet over a medium-high heat.Add mushrooms and cook until golden brown, about 6-8 minutes

  3. Stir in garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minutes

  4. Pour in heavy cream, then add parmesan, thyme, salt and pepper.Simmer until slightly thickened.

  5. Toss the drained pasta into th sauce, adding reserved pasta water as needed to loosen.Mix well to coat.

  6. Garnish with parsley and extra parmesan.Serve hot
